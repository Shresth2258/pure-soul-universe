Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know first.

Clear answers for planning your visit, program or retreat.

Where is Pure Soul Universe located?

Farm No. 100, Silver Oaks Lane No. 3, opposite Metro Pillar No. 146, Mehrauli–Gurugram Road, Ghitorni Village, New Delhi – 110030.

What are your working hours?

Our listed working hours are 5:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day. Please contact us before travelling so your visit can be confirmed.

How do I book a visit or program?

Call or WhatsApp +91 88003 16274, email psuwellnessandresorts01@gmail.com, or use the Contact page. A booking is complete only after our team confirms it in writing.

Are the programs suitable for beginners?

Beginner-friendly formats may be available. Tell us about your previous experience and intention, and we will suggest an appropriate starting point.

Which programs are currently available?

Program formats, facilitators and dates can change. Contact our team for the current list, availability, inclusions and fees.

Do you organise private or group retreats?

Tailored private and group formats may be planned for families, friends, communities or teams, subject to dates, capacity and program availability.

What should I bring?

Comfortable modest clothing, personal essentials and an open, unhurried mindset are a good start. Specific requirements will be shared with your confirmed program.

Are wellness services a substitute for medical treatment?

No. Our offerings are for general wellbeing, reflection and education. They do not diagnose, treat or cure medical or mental-health conditions and do not replace qualified professional care.

What is the cancellation or refund policy?

Applicable payment, cancellation and refund terms will be shared in writing before a booking is confirmed or payment requested.

How can I get the exact location?

The Contact page includes an address-based Google Maps search. You can also ask our team on WhatsApp for the latest location pin and arrival instructions.

Still curious? We are here to help.
