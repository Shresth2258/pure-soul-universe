About this therapy

What is Weight Management?

The Weight Management program combines personalised diet planning, Panchakarma therapies, yoga and counselling. It aims at gradual, sustainable change rather than a quick fix.

Benefits

Benefits of Weight Management

  • ✓Personalised plan for your body type
  • ✓Balanced, practical diet guidance
  • ✓Therapies such as Udvartana to support the plan
  • ✓Yoga and activity matched to you
  • ✓Counselling for habits and motivation
  • ✓Regular follow-up for steady progress

Timing & frequency

When and how often should it be done?

Duration

Initial consultation 45–60 minutes, with regular reviews

How often

A program of 4–12 weeks with follow-ups every 1–2 weeks

Best time

Start any time of the year; a consistent routine matters most.

Best suited for

People who want to manage weight in a healthy way, with support for diet, activity and lifestyle

The exact schedule is decided by our doctor after understanding your constitution and health.

The experience

How is it done?

  1. 1

    Consultation

    The doctor reviews your health, habits and goals.

  2. 2

    Diet plan

    A personalised diet chart is prepared.

  3. 3

    Therapies

    Suitable therapies are planned as part of the program.

  4. 4

    Yoga and activity

    Simple yoga and movement are included.

  5. 5

    Review

    Progress is reviewed and the plan is adjusted.

Good to know

Precautions & who should check first

  • Share any health condition, such as thyroid or diabetes, with the doctor
  • Not a crash-diet program
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women need medical advice first
  • Results depend on consistency and individual factors

This is a traditional wellbeing practice and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
