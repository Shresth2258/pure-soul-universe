About this therapy

What is Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda?

Also called Navara Kizhi, this therapy uses boluses of Shashtika (Navara) rice cooked in milk and herbal decoction. Warm boluses are massaged on the body to nourish the muscles and skin.

Benefits

Benefits of Shashtika Shali Pinda Sweda

  • ✓Nourishes muscles and tissues
  • ✓Supports strength and stamina
  • ✓Softens and nourishes the skin
  • ✓Calms the body and mind
  • ✓Rejuvenates after long fatigue
  • ✓Supports recovery and wellbeing

Timing & frequency

When and how often should it be done?

Duration

45–60 minutes

How often

Daily for 14–21 days, or as advised by the doctor

Best time

Morning, on a light stomach.

Best suited for

People with muscle weakness, tiredness, dry skin or those needing rejuvenation

The exact schedule is decided by our doctor after understanding your constitution and health.

The experience

How is it done?

  1. 1

    Rice preparation

    Navara rice is cooked in milk and herbal decoction.

  2. 2

    Bolus making

    The rice is tied into warm cloth boluses.

  3. 3

    Oil and massage

    After oil application, the boluses are massaged over the body.

  4. 4

    Warm bolus change

    Boluses are dipped back in warm milk to keep a steady heat.

  5. 5

    Rest

    The body is wiped and you rest for a few minutes.

Good to know

Precautions & who should check first

  • Consult the doctor if you have diabetes, kidney issues or high blood pressure
  • Not suitable during fever or acute illness
  • Tell us about milk or rice allergies
  • Avoid cold exposure after the session

This is a traditional wellbeing practice and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
