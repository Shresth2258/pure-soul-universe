About this therapy

What is Churna Pinda Sweda?

Churna Pinda Sweda is a warm massage using bundles filled with powdered herbs, chosen to suit the person. The dry heat and herbal powders offer comfort and deep relaxation.

Benefits

Benefits of Churna Pinda Sweda

  • ✓Gives deep, dry warmth and relaxation
  • ✓Eases stiffness and heaviness
  • ✓Supports comfort in muscles and joints
  • ✓Stimulates circulation
  • ✓Herbs chosen to suit your constitution
  • ✓Leaves the body light

Timing & frequency

When and how often should it be done?

Duration

40–60 minutes

How often

Daily for 7–14 days, as advised by the doctor

Best time

Morning, on a light stomach.

Best suited for

People with body stiffness, heaviness or muscle pain who prefer a dry-heat therapy

The exact schedule is decided by our doctor after understanding your constitution and health.

The experience

How is it done?

  1. 1

    Herb selection

    The doctor selects herbal powders for your needs.

  2. 2

    Preparing the bundles

    Powders are warmed and tied into cloth bundles.

  3. 3

    Massage

    The warm bundles are rolled and pressed over the body.

  4. 4

    Relax

    You rest while the warmth settles.

  5. 5

    Wash

    A warm bath is taken afterwards.

Good to know

Precautions & who should check first

  • Not for fever, skin infection or open wounds
  • Heat-sensitive people should say so before starting
  • Consult the doctor in pregnancy
  • Avoid cold exposure after the session

This is a traditional wellbeing practice and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
