Our story

A bridge between ancient wisdom and modern wellbeing.

Pure Soul Universe is a place to look within, reconnect with nature and explore a more harmonious way of living.

The founder

A vision shaped by service.

Pure Soul Universe is the visionary concept of founder Mr. Anil K. Jain.

A spiritualist committed to serving society, he envisions bringing together holistic spiritual therapies and wellness practices inspired by the profound knowledge of our ancestors and Rishis.

For centuries, these seekers explored the deeper dimensions of life through meditation, sadhana and spiritual practice. Pure Soul Universe carries that spirit forward for a new generation.

Our endeavour

Preserve. Rediscover. Share.

We aspire to bring the knowledge, wisdom and dedicated work of our ancestors to the new generation. Our traditions encompass meditation and sadhana, Mantras, Tantras and Yantras, alongside disciplines such as astronomy, astrology, numerology and face reading.

These are approached as traditional pathways for reflection, learning and self-discovery, with respect for their origins and the needs of the modern human being.

Our Vision & Mission

Guided by compassion. Rooted in tradition. Focused on a healthier, happier tomorrow.

01 — Vision

Timeless wisdom, carried forward.

To revive the wisdom of our Rishis, sages, masters and ancestors and help a new generation reconnect with the inner self, nature and deeper dimensions of life—moving consciously from dis-ease towards happiness, harmony and complete wellness.

A healthier, happier you

02 — Mission

Knowledge made meaningful today.

To preserve, explore and bring together meditation, Mantras, Tantras, Yantras, Sadhanas and holistic therapies in a responsible contemporary form that supports physical, mental, emotional and spiritual wellbeing.
