A possible rhythm

A day at Pure Soul Universe.

Each retreat is customised. This illustrative flow shows the kind of unhurried experience
we can create together.

  1. Morning

    Arrival & grounding

    Settle into the space and begin with breath,
    stillness or a nature-connected practice.

  2. Midday

    Learning & reflection

    Explore a chosen theme through guided meditation,
    traditional knowledge or a holistic wellbeing session.

  3. Afternoon

    Rest & reconnection

    Make space for personal rest, mindful conversation
    and time in a calm natural environment.

  4. Evening

    Integration & close

    Gather insights and return to everyday life with
    something simple to carry forward.
