Privacy Policy.

How Pure Soul Universe handles information shared through this website and our enquiry channels.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pure Soul Universe Wellness & Resort (“Pure Soul Universe”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal information when you visit this website, contact us or enquire about a visit, program, retreat or other service.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information handled through this website and our direct communication channels, including phone, email and WhatsApp. Third-party websites and apps have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide: When you contact us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, preferred dates, number of guests, interests, enquiry details, feedback and any other information included in your message.

Technical information: Our hosting and security providers may process limited technical data needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser or device details, timestamps, requested pages and diagnostic logs.

Cookies and analytics: We do not currently use first-party advertising cookies or a first-party analytics service on this website. External services may process information under their own policies when you interact with them.

3. How we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • respond to questions and communicate with you;
  • help plan a visit, program, retreat or stay;
  • confirm availability, bookings and related arrangements;
  • manage guest service, feedback and operational records;
  • maintain website and communication security; and
  • meet applicable legal or dispute-resolution requirements.

Where applicable, we rely on your consent or another basis permitted by law. You may withdraw consent for future processing by contacting us, subject to legal and operational requirements.

4. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share it only as reasonably necessary with service providers supporting website hosting, communications, guest arrangements or professional advice; with facilitators or partners involved in a service you request; or when required to protect rights, safety or comply with law.

This website links to or uses WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Google Maps and Google Fonts. Their operators may collect information and apply their own privacy policies. Some providers may process data outside India in accordance with applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including operational, accounting, legal or dispute-resolution needs. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to provide information about our processing, correct or update your details, erase information that is no longer required, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it.

7. Children’s information

Our website is not directed at children. A parent or lawful guardian should contact us and provide any necessary information for a person under 18. Please do not submit a child’s personal information without appropriate authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date.

9. Contact and grievances

For a privacy request, question or grievance, contact psuwellnessandresorts01@gmail.com or +91 88003 16274. Postal address: Farm No. 100, Silver Oaks Lane No. 3, opposite Metro Pillar No. 146, Mehrauli–Gurugram Road, Ghitorni Village, New Delhi – 110030.
