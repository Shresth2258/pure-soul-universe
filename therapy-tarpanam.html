About this therapy

What is Tarpanam?

Tarpanam is an Ayurvedic eye therapy in which medicated ghee is held around the eyes within a dough ring. It cools and nourishes the eyes and soothes eye strain.

Benefits

Benefits of Tarpanam

  • ✓Relieves eye strain and fatigue
  • ✓Soothes dry and tired eyes
  • ✓Cools and nourishes the eyes
  • ✓Supports eye comfort in screen users
  • ✓Relaxes the area around the eyes
  • ✓Supports restful sleep

Timing & frequency

When and how often should it be done?

Duration

15–30 minutes

How often

Daily for 3–7 days, or as advised by the doctor

Best time

Morning or evening, away from direct sun and wind.

Best suited for

People who spend long hours on screens, or have tired, dry or strained eyes

The exact schedule is decided by our doctor after understanding your constitution and health.

The experience

How is it done?

  1. 1

    Face massage

    A short face and head massage relaxes the area.

  2. 2

    Dough ring

    Dough rings are placed around both eyes.

  3. 3

    Medicated ghee

    Warm ghee fills the ring while you keep the eyes gently closed or open as guided.

  4. 4

    Eye movements

    You blink slowly as advised by the therapist.

  5. 5

    Cleaning

    Ghee is drained and the face is wiped.

Good to know

Precautions & who should check first

  • Not advised in acute eye infection, redness or fever
  • Avoid in cold or heavy congestion
  • Avoid direct wind and bright light after the session
  • Do not do this without the doctor's advice

This is a traditional wellbeing practice and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.
